## Strategy definitions you must respect (from the formalized strategy 大小周期共振)

- **T011 大周期方向判定 (major_timeframe_direction)** — "Judge the current trend direction on the major timeframe; return exactly one of long / short / uncertain. This is *following*, not forecasting: an ongoing up-move is 'long', an ongoing down-move is 'short'; do not try to call where a trend starts or ends. On 'uncertain' open no new position; existing positions keep being managed by the normal rules unless the uncertainty is judged a high-confidence reversal (then the position is closed). Inputs may be major-timeframe bars and any indicator or information grounded in that time scale."
- **T005 放弃判断行情性质** — Never classify the market as trending vs ranging and never use that as a filter; answer only "which direction is the current major-timeframe state", not "is this a trend".
- **T021 趋势反转 (major_trend_reversal)** — "While holding, judge on the major timeframe whether the trend has reversed; yes means close actively without waiting for the stop. Reversal means either that the direction has flipped to the opposite of the position, or that the direction is 'uncertain' and that uncertainty is of high confidence. A low-confidence 'uncertain' is not a reversal."
- **T010 大周期** — the major timeframe here is the daily scale (A1 bars are daily; A2/A3/A4 evidence is daily-or-slower). Minor-timeframe entry timing is NOT your job; do not reason about intraday timing.
- The output feeds a trend-following system with a 30% hit rate and ≥3:1 payoff expectation (T007): being wrong on direction is normal and cheap (tight stops), being systematically late or biased is expensive. Prefer 'uncertain' over a forced call when the major-timeframe state genuinely has no direction.
